Generated by `scripts/selection_stat.py` from the stored v0.11 design-period files (`results/v11/portfolio_design.csv`, `trials.json`); sample 2016-01-05..2021-12-31, 1564 daily periods, 260 periods a year.

**Selection statistic: deflated Sharpe of desk minus vol-target control = 0.009** at 81 registered trials (`len(evaluation.TRIALS)`).

The differential series is `desk - k * control` with `k = sd(desk) / sd(control) = 0.849`: the control levered to the desk's volatility, so a positive mean means a higher Sharpe than the control, and the desk's lower exposure is not counted against it.
